Company Overview
----------------
Birlasoft Limited (NSE: BSOFT, BSE: 532400) is a global IT services company
headquartered in Noida, India. A CK Birla Group company, Birlasoft provides
digital transformation, enterprise solutions, and cloud services to clients
across manufacturing, BFSI, energy & utilities, and life sciences verticals.
The company operates across 12 countries with 13,000+ employees.

Industry: Information Technology Services & Consulting
Sub-sector: Mid-cap IT Services
Peer Group: NIIT Technologies, Persistent Systems, Mphasis, Coforge, L&T Technology Services

================================================================================
                        NOTES & ACCOUNTING POLICIES
================================================================================

1. Revenue Recognition: Ind AS 115 — Fixed-price contracts recognized on
   percentage-of-completion basis; T&M contracts recognized as services rendered.

2. Foreign Currency: Functional currency is INR. Foreign currency transactions
   translated at spot rate; monetary assets/liabilities restated at closing rate.
   Forward contracts designated as cash flow hedges under Ind AS 109.

3. Employee Benefits: Gratuity and leave encashment as per actuarial valuation.
   ESOP expense recognized at fair value on grant date (Black-Scholes model).

4. Depreciation: PPE on straight-line basis over useful life per Schedule II.
   Intangibles (acquired software, customer relationships) amortized over 3-7 years.

5. The company has operating lease commitments of INR 2,800 Mn over next 5 years.

6. Related party transactions during FY25 include management remuneration of
   INR 180 Mn and transactions with CK Birla Group entities of INR 450 Mn.
